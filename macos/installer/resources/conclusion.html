OpenLW is installed.

  1. Connect the Mac to the Livewire network. The interface is selected automatically when a Livewire device is detected.
  2. Open OpenLW from Applications.
  3. To transmit Mac audio, choose OpenLW as the output, enter the channel, and enable transmission (currently labeled Diffuser in the app).
  4. To record a Livewire source, click its cell in the input matrix, then record from OpenLW.

To uninstall, open OpenLW and choose its uninstall command (currently OpenLW > Désinstaller OpenLW).
